## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 1. 🌟 Full 3D Interactive World (Three.js & WebGL)
- **Multi-tiered 3D Cake**: Chocolate & vanilla velvet cake with gold piping, decorative pearls, and a golden plaque reading *"ABUZAR • 2 NOVEMBER"*.
- **Interactive Candles**: Real-time flickering flames with dynamic warm point lights. Click the **"Blow Candles"** button to blow them out (with rising smoke physics trails) or relight them!
- **3D Cake Cutting Ceremony**: Click **"Cut Cake"** to watch a 3D chef knife float down, slice into the cake, slide the cake slice outward, and launch celebratory fireworks!
- **Interactive Floating 3D Metallic Balloons**: Shiny balloons floating and swaying around the cake. **Click or tap any balloon to POP it!** Spawns 3D fragments and confetti, updates your balloon pop score, and respawns after a few seconds.
- **3D Mystery Gift Box**: Positioned right on the pedestal. Click it directly in 3D to watch the lid flip open with golden light beams and reveal **Birthday VIP Privileges & Vouchers**!
- **3D Sky Fireworks**: Realistic projectile rockets launching into the sky and bursting into multicolored shimmering spark showers with gravity physics.

### 2. 🎵 Built-in Synthesizer Celebration Music & SFX (Web Audio API)
- 100% synthesized background melody of *"Happy Birthday"* with drums, bass, and chime chords.
- Real-time sound effects for:
  - Balloon pop 🎈
  - Candle blowing 🕯️
  - Knife slicing cake 🍰
  - Fireworks launch & explosions 🎆
  - Gift box unboxing chime ✨
- Zero external MP3 dependencies — works completely offline and instantly without broken audio links or CORS errors!

## 🚀 How to Run

### Method 1 (Direct Double Click):
Simply double-click `start.bat` or open `index.html` directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or any modern web browser!

### Method 2 (Local Server via Python):
In terminal / command prompt:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8000`
